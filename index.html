01THE INVISIBLE CRAFT

Readers never notice good spacing.
They always feel bad spacing.

Letter-spacing and line-height decide how fast the eye moves, where it rests and how easily it finds the next line. Get them wrong, and even a beautiful typeface feels cramped, noisy or tiring.

TRACKING · LETTER-SPACING

Air between letters

Tracking sets the space between every character. Display sizes want it tighter, small labels want it looser — one value never fits every size.

LEADING · LINE-HEIGHT

Room between lines

Too little and lines collide; too much and the eye loses its way back to the next line. Body text reads best between 1.4× and 1.6× its size.

Body 1.4–1.6×Headlines 1.0–1.2×

When lines sit too close, the eye loses its path and the paragraph turns into a dense grey block that is hard to follow from one line to the next.

Rhythm, not rules
